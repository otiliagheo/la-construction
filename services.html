Changes to a home in progress

For buyers interested before construction is complete, selected finishes or alterations may be possible, depending on the project stage, cost, and feasibility.

In certain circumstances, L&A may consider building for a committed buyer from the outset. These arrangements are evaluated individually and are not our standard business model.
