OUR BUSINESS

FAMILY-OWNED
AND OPERATED.

L&A Construction is operated by Adrian Gheorghe and Lea Sanciuc, a husband-and-wife team of Romanian heritage. They have three children and run the business together.

Adrian oversees construction and renovations. Lea represents the properties L&A brings to market. They generally begin projects independently rather than accepting commissioned work. Buyers may request changes to homes in progress when feasible; buyer-first builds are considered occasionally.
