02 / HOW WE OPERATEBUILDER FIRST · BUYER SECOND

WE BUILD
FIRST.

L&A typically selects the property, completes the construction or renovation, and then offers the home to buyers. Our projects are not normally started from customer requests.

OPTIONS FOR BUYERS

When a buyer connects with us before a home is finished, we may consider selected alterations depending on timing and feasibility. Occasionally, L&A may consider a buyer-first build on a case-by-case basis.
